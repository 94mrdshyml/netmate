# 29. Invite welcome screen (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/invite-welcome. Cut from fresh main after #48 (sign-up status emails) merged and deployed.
- Owner request: when a new person opens an invite link, show a welcome with the app's name and logo, "XYZ invited you to join <app>", three or four faces stacked on each other, and a short friendly line naming real members ("john, maria, sam and others are already here"), worded more attractively. DESIGN.md read first; the taste skill used inside its hard limits (flat, one brand colour, instance tokens, no emoji or em dashes).
- Decisions:
  - The link stays `/sign-up?invite=CODE` (links already shared keep working, and `/invite` is the member's own "Invite people" screen). A valid link now shows the welcome; its button goes to `/sign-up?invite=CODE&join=1`, which is the old form. A wrong or revoked link shows the same "Invite link not valid" message as before.
  - Who is shown: the inviter first, then the earliest active members who are not private, four faces in all, three first names said out loud, the rest counted as "N others". First names and picture keys only; no username, last name or address. The inviter is shown even if private because their name is on the screen anyway. This is the one place a visitor with no account sees members, so the privacy rule is in the query and tested.
  - Line: "{names} is/are already here, saving you a seat." (one name is singular; "1 other" and "N others"). No emoji, no em dash.
  - Look: the face row overlaps by 14px with a ring in the page colour, so it works on any page colour; faces settle in one after another (320ms, 70ms apart), off under reduced motion. Button is the existing full-width primary (48px).
- Code: `src/lib/welcome-line.ts`, `src/server/invite-welcome.ts` (`inviteWelcome`), `app/(app)/sign-up/invite-welcome.tsx`, `page.tsx` (`join` param), CSS in `globals.css`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/welcome-line.test.ts` (6), `tests/integration/invite-welcome.test.ts` (6: order and caps, others count, Pending and private never shown, only first name and picture key leave the server, inactive inviter gives nothing), `tests/e2e/invite.spec.ts` (+2: welcome content, faces 2 to 4, line, button opens the form; phone fit and tap size). Every e2e that signs up through an invite link now goes past the welcome through `openSignUpForm` in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts` (helpers, invite, hq, rate-limit specs). That is the flow changing on purpose; no test was skipped, loosened or deleted. Full e2e at 1 worker: 97 passed. Looked at in light and dark at 390px.
- Open: the owner may want an instance setting to turn the faces off (a private community might not want even first names shown to someone holding a leaked link); not built. Next: invite by email (own PR), then forgot and reset password, then verification and welcome emails.
