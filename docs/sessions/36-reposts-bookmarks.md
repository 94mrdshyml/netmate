# 36. Reposts, quotes and bookmarks (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/reposts-bookmarks, PR below once opened. Owner asked for "1 then 2" from the list of what is next: this is item 1 (reposts, quotes, bookmarks); hashtags and mentions are the next PR.
- Done: a repost button under each post (a small sheet: Repost or Undo repost, and Quote), a bookmark button, a "You reposted" / "Meera Rao reposted" line above a reposted post, a quote card under a quote post, `/compose?quote=<id>`, the Saved posts screen (`/bookmarks`, a row in Settings), repost and quote alerts (the alert icons for them were placeholders and are now a repeat arrow and a pencil).
- How: migration `0013_reposts_bookmarks.sql`: tables `reposts` and `bookmarks` (person + post is the key, indexed by person and time), and two columns on `posts` added by hand in the SQL (`quoted_post_id`, with ON DELETE SET NULL and a CHECK that a post is not both a reply and a quote; `repost_count`, never negative). Drizzle would rebuild the whole `posts` table to add the CHECKs and drops the ON DELETE rule, and a rebuild risks cascade deletes on D1, as with `place` in 0011. Forward-only, safe on older instances. `src/server/posts.ts`: `hydrate` fills photos, GIF and the quote card in one pass; `setRepost` (row, counter and alert in one `db.batch()`); `createPost` takes the quoted post id as its 8th argument. The feed and a profile merge posts and reposts with `mergedPage`: both lists are read newest first with the same (time, post id) cursor, merged, cut to a page, and the cursor is the last entry, so paging never loses or repeats an item. Several reposts of one post on a page show once. `src/server/bookmarks.ts`: `setBookmark`, `listBookmarks`. `src/server/notifications.ts`: `alertOnRepost`, `removeRepostAlert`, `alertOnQuote`. Rate limits 30 reposts and 60 saves a minute.
- Rules kept: a private account's posts can be neither reposted nor quoted, not even by their owner (the button is hidden and the server refuses); a reply can be quoted but not reposted; a quote carries no pictures or GIF (the server refuses the mix); taking a repost back, or un-saving, always works even if the post is now hidden from you; saving a post you cannot see is refused; the Saved list goes through `visiblePosts`; no bookmark count exists in any type or page; a muted person's reposts, and reposts of a muted person's post, are out of the home feed but still show on a profile you open on purpose.
- Tests: `tests/integration/reposts.test.ts` (20: counters and idempotence, own post, refusals, taking back after a block, alerts and grouping, feed visibility for followers, muted, deleted, private and blocked; paging across mixed posts and reposts; profile; database checks; cascade on account removal), `tests/integration/quotes-bookmarks.test.ts` (13: card, empty quote, feed and search, alert, "not available" for a blocked, private or deleted original, every refusal with nothing written, place, quote of a reply; bookmarks private, ordered, paged, skipped when hidden, refused when not visible), `tests/e2e/reposts-bookmarks.spec.ts` (4: repost and undo with "You reposted" and the post twice; quote flow and card link, 404 for a missing post; saving with no count, Saved posts list and removal; no repost button on a private account, with the switch put back). Named so it runs after `posts.spec`; two admin posts and no sign-ups.
- Not done: Share (the phone's share sheet or copy link; PRD 8.2); email for alerts; "X and 2 others quoted" is not grouped (quotes are separate posts); the For you ranking (reposts count in it later); analytics counts of saves.
