# 49. Paid subscriptions, step 1: connect Polar and set the split (2026-10-24)
- Branch / PR: feat/polar-connection. Owner's words: "next we need subscriptions. users can setup. Set a price and others can subscribe. Subscription would require a privacy option in the posts. All or subscribers. Admin will setup a split ratio ... Can we use polar.sh for this". Answers: Polar, money to the instance owner's account, payout automation later; the instance owner carries the money and tax paperwork; the owner left "who pays the fees" and "refunds" to me; old subscriber-only posts are NOT kept visible after a subscription ends.
- What I found out about Polar (from its docs, 2026-10-24): it takes the payment and handles sales tax (a "merchant of record"), but it pays out to one account per organisation. It does not split a payment between several people, so the member's share is tracked by us and paid by the owner. Fee: 4% plus 40 cents, plus 0.5% on subscriptions (newer accounts may pay 5% plus 50 cents). Webhooks use the Standard Webhooks signature (headers webhook-id, webhook-timestamp, webhook-signature). Checkout: `POST /v1/checkouts/` (products, metadata, external_customer_id, success_url). Product: `POST /v1/products/` (recurring_interval month, a fixed USD price in cents). Cancel at period end: `PATCH /v1/subscriptions/{id}` with `cancel_at_period_end`. Order money fields: subtotal, discount, net (after discount, before tax), tax, total, refunded, refunded_tax. I did not read Polar's pages for every event payload, so step 2 must check the exact webhook payload fields against a real sandbox event before it is trusted.
- My two decisions: (1) the member earns a percent of the payment before tax; Polar's fee comes out of the owner's side, so a member always knows what they earn and the owner can read the effect on the page; (2) a refund takes the member's share back in proportion. Both are one pure function (`creatorShareCents` in `src/lib/money.ts`) so they are easy to change.
- The plan is four pull requests: (1) this one; (2) set a price, Subscribe, webhooks, subscriptions and payment ledger; (3) subscriber-only posts and the visibility rule; (4) earnings and manual payouts. It is written out in PRD 8.14.
- Done in step 1:

  - Money rules, tested: `src/lib/money.ts` (whole cents, $1 to $100 a month, the split, rounding down, refunds, never more paid out than received).
  - Webhook signature check, tested with both key forms Polar has used, wrong bodies, old and future timestamps and malformed headers: `src/payments/webhook.ts`. Polar client with plain fetch (no new package): `src/payments/polar.ts`.
