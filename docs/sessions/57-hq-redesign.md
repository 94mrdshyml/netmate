# 57. Admin panel redesign (2026-10-24)
- Branch / PR: feat/hq-redesign. The owner: "redo the UI/UX, not just redesign the screens, first analyse and club together similar things. Start with HQ. Full enterprise grade SaaS feel." Answers: five groups, Revenue and Payments merged into one Money section with tabs, dense and professional, one PR for all of HQ.
- Analysis: the old menu had a catch-all "Community" group (Branding, Email, Payments, Revenue, Services, Demo data) that mixed three jobs; Payments and Revenue were one subject on two pages; Activity and Revenue both showed numbers differently; four people-and-case lists each had their own search and chip layout.
- Done: menu in five groups (Home, People, Insights, Money, Settings). Money is one menu item with three tabs: Overview (`/hq/money`, what was Revenue), Members and payouts (`/hq/money/payouts`) and Polar setup (`/hq/money/setup`, what was Payments). The old `/hq/payments` and `/hq/revenue` addresses check the admin and then send them on. Breadcrumbs now show the group. New quick search in the top strip (`/` or Ctrl+K, Enter to jump, can also look a person up in Members). One toolbar for the Members and Reports lists, a compact member row (four numbers, "All numbers" folds the rest), sticky table headers, hover on rows, number panels two to a row on a phone, field width and panel spacing. The Overview now has open reports and what members are owed, each linking to its page, and "Needs you" gained Reports and Payouts rows.
- Tests: new unit test for the page list and `sectionFor`; new browser spec `hq-structure.spec.ts` (five groups, Money tabs and breadcrumbs, old addresses, quick search by keyboard and on a phone, member row). Existing browser tests only changed where the page they open moved: `payments.spec.ts` and `subscriptions.spec.ts` use `/hq/money/setup`, `/hq/money` and `/hq/money/payouts` and the heading is now "Money"; `hq.spec.ts` also checks the new addresses and the old ones for 404 to non-admins. No test was removed or loosened.
- Not done: the Activity page, Invites, Approvals, Services, Email and Branding keep their current layouts apart from the shared toolbar, spacing and table styling. A deeper pass (a settings-row layout, real sortable tables) would be a next step.
- Next: the member side of the app (Settings, profile, subscriptions) gets the same analyse-then-club treatment, only on the owner's go. Subscriber-only posts (step 3) are still waiting for the owner's go.
