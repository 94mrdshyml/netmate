# 46. Members, moderators and numbers (2026-10-24)
- Branch / PR: feat/members-roles. The owner asked for an admin page with all users, promoting people to admin or moderator, basic numbers (posts, replies, reposts, GIFs, pictures, links, interactions), and what else the admin panel needs before the first release.
- Conflicts, asked and answered: the spec says there is exactly one admin (the database enforces it) and lists multiple admins as not in version 1, and there was no moderator role or any moderation tool. The owner chose: **one owner plus moderators**. No second admin. A moderator may remove posts and replies, suspend members, and see the members list and numbers; not approve sign-ups.
- This round (1 of 3): the Members page, the Activity page and making or removing a moderator. Moderators cannot yet remove or suspend anything: those tools come next round, so for now a moderator only sees the two pages.
- Next rounds, in order: (2) suspend and unsuspend a member, remove a post or reply with a short reason, remove a member for good (admin only), and an activity log of who did what; (3) reports from members, a community rules page and an announcement banner, site health (storage and database size, email, scheduled jobs), and a backup and export guide.
- How: a moderator is a flag on the profile (`profiles.is_moderator`, 0 or 1, migration 0016 with a hand-written CHECK), not a new role, so the database rule that allows one admin is untouched and older instances upgrade safely. `requireStaff()` (admin or moderator) guards only `/hq/members` and `/hq/stats`; `/hq` sends a moderator to the list; every other page and action keeps `requireAdmin()` and gives a moderator a plain 404. The menu shows a moderator only their two pages. Settings shows a moderator a "Moderation" row.
- Numbers: per person (posts, replies, quotes, reposts, pictures, GIFs, link cards, likes given and received, followers, following) and for the whole community, plus the last eight blocks of seven days. Counts only; **saves are left out entirely** (bookmarks are private, CLAUDE.md rule 2). A moderator sees no email addresses and cannot search by email. Per-post rankings (best posts) are left for the analytics phase, because they need the visibility rule and views.
- Tests: unit (`roles.test.ts`); integration (`members-admin.test.ts`, 9): exact per-person counts for every kind, emails only for the admin, search without case and with % and _ taken literally, filters, paging without repeats, moderator rules (idempotent, not the admin, not someone waiting, not a stranger, database refuses 2), the weekly numbers (a 9-day-old post lands in the week before, a 100-day-old post and 90-day-old member are outside, someone waiting is not a new member), and no mention of saves anywhere. Browser (`hq.spec.ts`): plain members get 404 on both pages, and a full run where the admin makes a real member a moderator, who then sees the list (no emails, no buttons), the Activity page and the Moderation row, gets 404 on every other admin page, and loses it all when the admin takes the role back.
- Not done: a way to hand the owner role to someone else; per-member "last active" (left out on purpose, it is a watching feature).
