# 39. Pictures and GIFs on quotes (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/quote-media, PR below once opened. The owner asked for "images and gifs on quotes too" (with the profile post count, session 38). Built on the hashtags branch (PR 60, `createPost` was changed there too) and opened after it merged.
- Decision (owner, overrides PRD 8.2 which said a post has photos, or a GIF, or a quote): a quote post may carry its own pictures (up to 4) or one GIF, like any post. Also, the quote card now shows the original's picture or GIF instead of the words "With 2 pictures". I read "images and gifs on quotes" as both; if only one was meant, the other is a small revert.
- Done: the composer keeps the picture and GIF buttons when quoting; the post shows its own pictures or GIF first and the quote card below. The card shows the original's first picture (cropped, at most 180px tall, "+2" when it has more) or its GIF (whole). The composer's preview of the original shows the same.
- How: `createPost` no longer refuses media with a quote (the `quote_with_media` code is gone, and its action message); a GIF with pictures is still refused. `QuotedView` carries `photos` and `gif` instead of `photoCount` and `hasGif`; `hydrate` reads the pictures and GIFs of the quoted posts in the same two queries as the posts' own (the card is still built only for an original this reader may see, so a hidden original shows no media). No migration.
- Tests changed on purpose (a rule changed by the owner, not weakened): the integration test that expected a quote with pictures or a GIF to be refused now expects it to be saved and shown (and the mix of pictures and a GIF still refused); the e2e test that expected no picture or GIF buttons while quoting now expects them. New: quote with pictures, picture only, GIF only, the original's pictures and GIF in the card, none for a reader who cannot see the original (`tests/integration/quotes-bookmarks.test.ts`); in the browser, the original's picture inside the composer's card, a quote with its own picture and the card's small picture loading (`reposts-bookmarks.spec.ts`), and a GIF on a quote of a GIF post (`search-gif.spec.ts`).
- Not done: showing every picture of the original in the card (only the first); opening a picture from the card (the whole card is a link to the original).
