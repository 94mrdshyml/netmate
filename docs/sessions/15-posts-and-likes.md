# 15. Posts, Home feed, likes and replies (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/posts-and-likes
- Done: the first real social features. Owner decisions this session: `/hq` waits until the basic app works; slices are combined as (1) posts, feed, likes and replies, (2) profiles, follows, bottom nav and Settings, (3) avatars; R2 keeps avatars, banners (cover images) and post pictures under separate prefixes (shipped first as PR #31, `src/storage/keys.ts`). Also: all 19 merged branches were deleted (local and remote) at the owner's request.
- What shipped: tables `posts` (prefix `post_`, body 1 to 280 by database check, reply and like counters, soft delete) and `likes` (one row per person per post) in migration `0003_posts_and_likes` (additive, forward-only). `src/server/visibility.ts` holds the one `visiblePosts` rule (not deleted, author is an active member); every read and the like check use it. `src/server/posts.ts`: `listFeed`, `getPost`, `listReplies`, `createPost` (post or reply), `deletePost` (own posts only; anyone else's is "not found"), `setLike` (wanted state, idempotent). A counter changes in the same `db.batch()` as the row, by recounting rows, so it is exact however often or concurrently it runs. Cursors are `(milliseconds, id)`; a forged cursor is ignored. UI: Home shows the feed (newest top-level posts, 20 per page, "Older posts"), a round compose button, `/compose` (character ring, disabled when empty or over 280), the post page `/[username]/status/[id]` with an inline reply box and replies, like toggle with instant feedback, delete with confirmation, relative times. Text is always rendered as text. Feather icons are drawn inline (no package). Posts are limited to 10 per minute and likes to 60 per minute per person with the exact D1 limiter.
- Tests: 20 new unit (post text rules incl. emoji counting, control characters, relative time), 24 new integration (post/reply rules, 280 chars and 280 emoji, markup stored unchanged, reply counter incl. 6 simultaneous repliers, delete rules, visibility, cursor paging with posts sharing a millisecond, forged cursor, idempotent like, 10 simultaneous likers, database CHECKs), 9 new e2e at phone width (post at top, composer limits and fit, text not run as markup, like persists, reply flow, delete with confirm, another member sees it and cannot delete it, not-found pages, signed-out redirect).
- Real problems found by tests: (1) after a reply the app sent the person to the reply's own page; it now stays on the thread, clears the box and shows the reply. (2) Lint (React purity and `<a>` links) forced `currentTime()` and `Link`. (3) A loose e2e locator matched two "Replies" labels.
- Decisions and changes to existing tests: the spike lines "D1 binding / R2 binding / Instance: configured" are gone from Home; the smoke test now checks the feed region and the setup test checks "Signed in as @username" (stronger). The R2 binding stays covered by integration tests. Home has one "latest posts" feed for now; the ranked For you and Following tabs come with follows (next PR). The invite link and Sign out still live on Home until Settings exists. Likes use the exact D1 limiter instead of the approximate Rate Limiting binding (correct, heavier) until that binding is set up.
- Open: no profile pages or links on names yet; no photos, GIFs, quotes, reposts, hashtags, mentions or notifications; no infinite scroll or new-posts pill; no admin delete of others' posts (comes with `/hq`).
- Next: profiles, follows, bottom navigation and Settings (one PR), then avatars.
