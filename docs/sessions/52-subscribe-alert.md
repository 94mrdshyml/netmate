# 52. An alert when someone subscribes (2026-10-24)
- Branch / PR: feat/subscribe-alert. The owner ran the real Polar test ("working") and asked for three things: (1) the member should be alerted when someone subscribes; (2) revenue and payouts calculated in `/hq`; (3) a member can see who their subscribers are and their revenue. This PR is (1). (2) and (3) are the next PR, kept separate because they are a different feature (money pages and a payouts table).
- Done: a new alert kind `subscribe` ("Meera subscribed to you"), sent to the creator once, the first time the subscription is paid up (active or trialing). `subscriptions.notified_at` is set and the alert written in one batch, and the alert is written only if that update changed a row (`changes()`), so repeated, late and out-of-order Polar messages cannot alert twice. A subscription that is never paid for never alerts; one that becomes active later alerts then; an order that arrives first (carrying its subscription) alerts too. Subscribing again later brings the old alert to the top as unread. Hidden for someone who blocked the subscriber. Tapping it opens `/subscription`.
- Migration 0020: `notifications` is rebuilt once to add the kind to its two checks (SQLite cannot change a check in place; nothing points at that table; every row and all four indexes are copied or recreated; I removed the `PRAGMA foreign_keys` lines drizzle writes). It also adds `subscriptions.notified_at` and marks subscriptions that already exist as told about, so existing test subscriptions do not alert late.
- Tests: unit (wording, no emoji or em dash), integration (6: once, not before payment, order-first, again later, blocked and removed, the database checks), browser (the creator sees the alert and follows it).
- Privacy note for the owner: the alert names the subscriber to the creator. The next PR also gives creators a list of their subscribers' names (the owner asked for that). Today the Subscribe box says people pay on Polar's page; I will add a line there that the person they subscribe to can see their name.
