# 44. Layout wider than the phone (2026-10-24)
- Branch / PR: fix/mobile-layout. The owner sent a phone screenshot (Chrome on Android, about 450px wide) of the live feed: the compose button hung past the right edge, the bottom bar's labels were cut off at the bottom, and the bars only showed fully after scrolling.
- Cause: the small "You reposted" line above a post was set to fill a whole row (`flex: 0 0 100%`) and also had a 52px left margin, so it stuck out 52px past the column on every page with a repost in it. A phone browser answers one element wider than the screen by making the whole layout viewport wider (here 487px on a 450px phone). The bottom bar and the compose button are `position: fixed`, so they were laid out for the wider screen: the button's right edge landed at 468px and the bar spanned about 484px, so both were partly off screen until the page was moved. Reproduced at 450px and 360px; every other page I checked (post page, search, alerts, saved, settings, profile) was fine.
- Fix: `.post-reposted` now takes the row minus its margin (`flex: 0 0 calc(100% - 52px)`), can shrink (`min-width: 0`) and breaks long names (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
- Why the old checks missed it: the existing tests compare `documentElement.scrollWidth` with `window.innerWidth`. On a phone `innerWidth` grows together with the overflow, so the difference is always zero. The new test (`tests/e2e/viewport-fit.spec.ts`) measures against the width of the phone itself (360, 390 and 412), on Home, Following, Search, Alerts, Settings and a post page, with a repost and a long unbroken web address in the feed; it also checks that the bottom bar and the compose button sit inside the screen. Without the fix it fails ("scrollWidth 396" on a 360px phone); with it, it passes. The old assertions are untouched (they pass, they just cannot fail for this reason).
- Docs: `DESIGN.md` hard limits now say nothing may be wider than the phone and how to size a full-row item that has a margin.
