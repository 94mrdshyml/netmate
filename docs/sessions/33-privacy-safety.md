# 33. Privacy and safety (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/privacy-safety, PR below once opened. Owner chose it from the options after asking what could be done beside the posts work another agent is doing.
- Done (PRD 8.8): private account and its follow requests, block, mute, and the one visibility rule that knows about them. Settings has a Privacy row (`/privacy`, `/privacy/requests`, `/privacy/blocked`, `/privacy/muted`). A "..." menu on other people's profiles has Mute and Block. The follow button has a third state, "Requested". A private profile you may not see shows the header and a lock; a blocked profile shows a plain message.
- How: migration `0009_privacy.sql` adds `follow_requests`, `blocks`, `mutes` (new tables only, forward-only, safe on older instances). `src/server/visibility.ts` now has `personVisible` (yourself, or an active member who is public or followed, with no block either way), `visiblePosts` built on it, plus `notBlockedWith` and `notMutedBy`. `src/server/privacy.ts` has the private switch, answering requests, block, mute and the three lists. Follow, followers lists, search, suggestions, the home feed and alerts were changed to use them. Everything a change implies (follows removed, alerts taken back) is in the same `db.batch()`.
- Decisions the PRD left open: muted people still show on their profile and in search (PRD only names the home feed, For you and alerts); unblocking does not bring a follow back; a declined person is not told and may ask again; approving does not send an extra "followed you" alert; a blocked person sees "You cannot see this profile." and cannot tell a block from anything else that hides it; the Mates line and the counts as links are hidden on a private profile you may not see. The actions for answering, blocking and muting do not call `revalidatePath`, because any call re-draws the page you are on and the row would vanish before it could say "Approved".
- API change: `setFollow` now answers `{ ok: true, state: "none" | "following" | "requested" }` instead of `following: boolean`, and `FollowButton` takes `state`. The four assertions in `follows.test.ts` were changed to the new shape and say the same thing.
- Tests: `tests/integration/privacy.test.ts` (21: every who-sees-what combination across posts, feed, replies, search and lists; the request flow, idempotence, "cannot force a follow", answered only by the account asked; turning private off; block removing follows, requests and alerts in one step; mute hiding feed and alerts only; limits and refusals; database checks and cascade). I broke the private rule and the block rule on purpose to check the tests fail (4 and 2 tests did). `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` (8 steps with two real members and the admin as a stranger: switch, lock, Requested, alert, approve, follower sees posts, mute, block with its confirm, search and profile for both sides, unblock, turning private off, phone width, signed-out).
- Sign-up budget: a whole e2e run may sign up 20 people through the admin's link (PRD 8.11: 20 per 10 minutes per link). After this change the suite uses all 20 (rate-limit.spec alone spends 10 on its flood test). privacy.spec spends only one, Priya; Sam and a stranger join through Priya's own link, which has its own count. Any new spec that needs a member must join through a member's link or move into a spec that already has members, or the last sign-ups will be refused.
- Not done, for whoever builds them: repost and quote of a private account's post must be refused (they should use `personVisible` and also check "not private"); the For you ranking must reuse the feed's mute filter; a reply on a private post still shows in the replier's own lists (it is the replier's public post).
- Open: member data export and account deletion (PRD 8.1) will have to remove blocks, mutes and requests; they already cascade with the user row.
- Next: owner picks the next feature.
