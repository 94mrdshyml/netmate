# 16. Profiles, follows, bottom navigation and Settings (2026-10-23)
- Branch / PR: feat/profiles-follows-settings
- Before this: PR #32 (posts and likes) is live and its deploy is green. The #31 deploy had failed once with a Cloudflare asset-upload 401 (wrangler retried 5 times and gave up); it did not repeat, and the live site was never affected.
- Done: `/[username]` profile (name, @username, bio, city or country, link, joined month, follower and following counts, the Mates line, Posts and Replies tabs, Follow or Edit profile). `/[username]/followers` and `/following` (paged, Follow buttons, no button on your own row). Follow and unfollow send the wanted state and are idempotent; 30 follows per 10 minutes per person (exact D1 limiter). Home now has For you and Following tabs; Following shows your posts and those of people you follow. `/profile` edits first name, last name, bio (160), city or country (60) and link (100, `riya.dev` becomes `https://riya.dev/`, only http and https with a real domain, no username:password, shown without the scheme, opens in a new tab with `nofollow`); the username is read only. `/settings` (Invite people, Edit profile, Log out) and `/invite` (your link, copy, how many joined). Bottom bar: Home, Profile, Settings. Names on posts link to profiles. The invite link and Sign out moved off Home.
- Structure: member pages now live in the route group `app/(app)/(member)/`, whose layout sends signed-out visitors to sign in and draws the bottom bar. Migration `0004_follows`: one table (pair is the primary key; the database refuses following yourself) and two indexes; additive, forward-only. Reserved usernames gained `profile` and `account` because those are now static routes (PRD list updated).
- Tests: 20 new integration (follow idempotency, self and missing and waiting targets, 10 simultaneous follows leave one row, database refuses self-follow, counts and my own follow state, case-insensitive @username, Mates names and count, paging without losing anyone, forged cursor, Following feed contents and unfollow, posts versus replies tabs, profile edit rules, only your own profile changes), 21 unit for the link rules, and 7 new e2e at phone width (bottom bar and 44px targets, own profile, edit with all problems at once and the saved result, a new member following and unfollowing with the lists and feed changing, 404s, name links and Replies tab, Settings and the invite page).
- Real problems found by tests: (1) profile edit skipped the link check when another field was invalid (same class of bug as setup; fixed by validating each field independently). (2) Two e2e races of my own making: the follow and like buttons change instantly (optimistic) and the test navigated away before the server answered; tests now wait for the button to re-enable. (3) Parallel posting by the new social tests made "the first article" ambiguous; post tests now find their own post by its unique text.
- Changes to existing tests (relocations, not weakening): the invite link, "Signed in as" and Sign out left Home, so the invite, rate-limit, posts, sign-in and setup specs read the link from `/invite`, check the Profile link in the bottom bar, and log out from `/settings`. Shared helpers in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts`.
- Decisions: the bottom bar shows Home, Profile and Settings for now; Search and Alerts (PRD 8.13) join when those screens exist. "For you" is still the latest posts from everyone until the ranked feed. Counts on a profile are counted from the follows table, not stored.
- Open: no avatars or banners yet (next PR); no private accounts, requests, blocks or mutes (the one visibility rule in `src/server/visibility.ts` is where they plug in); no Mates block after three posts, no suggestions; no username check as you type; no theme toggle in a top bar; no city suggestions.
